# Governance

## Maintainer

The repository owner, `@varunkumar898`, is the initial Maintainer. The Maintainer sets project direction, reviews and merges pull requests, manages releases, and coordinates security response.

## How decisions are made

Discussion is open in issues and pull requests. The Maintainer makes the final decision when consensus is not practical, explaining the reasoning and keeping decisions focused on the project's scope, clean-room policy, and user needs.

## Path to shared responsibility

1. **Contributor**: submits focused changes, tests, and documentation and follows the project policies.
2. **Reviewer**: after a sustained record of thoughtful reviews and reliable contributions, the Maintainer may invite a contributor to review pull requests and help triage issues.
3. **Co-maintainer**: a reviewer may be invited to share release, repository, and moderation responsibilities after demonstrating sustained judgment, availability, and agreement with the project's policies.

These are invitations, not automatic promotions. Permissions are granted deliberately and can be changed if responsibilities change.

## Releases

The Maintainer prepares versioned releases and release notes. Each release should state its compatibility limits, supported platforms, verification performed, and any known security or licensing caveats.
