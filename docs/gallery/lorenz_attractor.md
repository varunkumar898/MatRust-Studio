# Chaotic Lorenz Strange Attractor (ode45)

**Area**: ODE  
**Script**: `examples/math/ode/lorenz_attractor.m`  

## Mathematical Background

Solves the 3D Lorenz non-linear atmospheric convection system (sigma=10, rho=28, beta=8/3) and renders the iconic butterfly attractor in 3D.

## Expected Numerical Outcomes & Verification

- **Expected Results**: Non-periodic chaotic trajectory bound inside butterfly lobes, z in [0, 50]
- All figures render with informative titles, axis labels, and legends where appropriate.

## Parameters to Vary

- Modify grid resolution, time spans, or solver options to explore system dynamics and stability.
- Adjust parameters to inspect sensitivity, convergence rates, and bifurcation transitions.
